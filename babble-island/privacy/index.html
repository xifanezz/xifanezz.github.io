Babble Island

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Babble Island is a game for iPhone and iPad that helps young children say their first English words and learn the alphabet. It is made by Mohammed Faqihi, an independent developer ("we").

In short

  • We receive nothing about your child, or about you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. It goes online only for the purchase, which is made with Apple alone: Apple takes the payment and tells the app on your device whether the full game is bought. Nothing about it reaches us.
  • What the game remembers stays on your device: the child's progress and, for the parent, short recordings of the child saying the game's words.
  • The microphone is used only during a "say the word" moment, and never in the background.
  • The parent's area of the game (settings and the Parent Corner) is behind a question for grown-ups.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps these in its private storage on the iPhone or iPad. It never sends them to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhy
Game progress: levels finished, stars, coins, the hero's look (boy or girl, colours, hats) and badges So the child continues where they left off
Practice per word: how often each word was found, tried and said, what the speech recogniser heard last, and counts per day To choose which words to practise next, and for the parent's weekly summary in the Parent Corner
Recordings of the child's voice: the newest 5 attempts for each word, as short audio files So the parent can listen to how the child says each word, in the Parent Corner. Older recordings are deleted automatically
Settings: volumes, mute, boy or girl, how strictly speech is judged To keep the parent's choices
A short technical log of the screens opened and memory use To help find the cause of a crash. It stays on the device
Whether the full game is bought on this Apple Account, or shared with it by a family member through Family Sharing So the game opens without a network

The app does not ask for, and does not store, the child's photo, contacts, location or any identifier. For the speech therapist's report a parent may type the child's first name and month of birth; they stay on the device.

Microphone and speech recognition

  • Before iOS asks for the microphone and speech recognition, the game shows a card for parents, behind a question for grown-ups. Without these permissions the game still works: the "say the word" moments are simply skipped. They can be turned on later from the settings.
  • The microphone opens only while the speech bubble with the microphone is on screen, for a few seconds. iOS shows its orange microphone indicator while it is open.
  • Speech is recognised by Apple's speech recognition on the device itself (the Speech framework built into iOS); the audio never leaves the device. On a device that cannot recognise the language on the device, the game skips recognition and simply listens for the child's attempt.
  • The recording of each attempt is saved only on the device, for the parent.

The purchase

  • Purchases are handled by Apple (the App Store, through Apple's StoreKit). Your payment details, name, email and address stay with Apple. We never receive them, and no other company takes part in the purchase.
  • The app asks Apple, on your device, whether this Apple Account has the full game: bought on it, or shared with it by a family member through Family Sharing. It keeps the answer on the device, so the game opens without a network. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Restore Purchases (on the purchase screen, and in Settings → “Purchase”) asks Apple again. On a new iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account it brings the full game back.
  • The full game is a one-time purchase, with nothing to cancel. Refunds are Apple's (reportaproblem.apple.com); a refunded purchase locks the islands again.

Children's privacy

Babble Island is made for young children to play with a parent. We do not collect personal information from children, and nothing is processed away from the device by us: the purchase is between a parent and Apple, and holds nothing about the child. No information is sold or used for advertising. This is how we meet children's privacy laws such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR and UK GDPR in Europe and the United Kingdom, and Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law.

The settings, the Parent Corner, the microphone request and the purchase (its card, its links and the payment itself) open only after a grown-up answers a sum. The child is only ever asked to call a parent; the child never sees a price.

Your control over the data

  • Listen: the Parent Corner shows the child's progress and plays their recordings.
  • Share: in the Parent Corner, “Report for the therapist” makes a PDF of the child's progress, with short recordings of their voice only if the parent ticks them, and opens the device's share sheet so the parent can choose who receives it; nothing is sent automatically.
  • Delete: in the Parent Corner, “Delete recordings” (on the Words page) deletes every recording of the child's voice and what the recogniser heard; “Start again” (on the Islands page) erases all progress as well. Deleting the app from the device deletes everything it stored, recordings included.
  • Backups: if you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is yours and is handled by Apple, not by us.

Changes

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated first and the date above will change. We will not start collecting data without asking a parent first.
