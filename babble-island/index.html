Babble Island

About the game

A 3D game for iPhone and iPad that helps young children say their first English words, numbers, colors and the alphabet, and trace every letter, with a warm, clear voice. The child builds a hero, sails from island to island, and every level is a different game.

The parents' area

On the map, press and hold the settings gear for one second, then answer the sum. There you'll find the volumes, boy or girl, how strictly speech is judged (easy / normal), turning on the microphone, the privacy policy, the credits and the Parent Corner.

The purchase

Fun Island, the first island, and its seven levels are free. To open every island: the full game, one purchase through your Apple account that is yours for good. No subscription, nothing renews.

A subscription from before: if you subscribed monthly or yearly before the game became a one-time purchase, cancel it on the iPhone or iPad in Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Babble Island, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

A new device or a reinstall: the purchase belongs to your Apple Account. On any iPhone or iPad signed in to it, open “Purchase” in the parents' area, then “Restore Purchases”. With Apple's Family Sharing, your family members get the full game on their own devices too.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Speech and the microphone

During a "say the word" moment the microphone opens for a few seconds only, and speech is recognised on the device itself, without the internet. If the word isn't understood, Rania says it again and the child tries once more; any real attempt on the second try counts.

The child's recordings

In the Parent Corner you can listen to the newest 5 attempts at each word. “Delete recordings” deletes them all; “Start again” also erases progress.
